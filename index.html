Greetings from JAHAN TRADERS.

M/S JAHAN TRADES is a leading Bangladeshi jute exporter and manufacturer established in 1974. With over 3 decades of experience, we have earned a reputation as a trusted global provider, exporting all kinds of quality jute and jute products worldwide.

We are exporting all kinds Jute Products as like as, FOOD GRADE JUTE BAG (VOT), HESSIAN CLOTH, HESSIAN BAGS, JUTE YARN / TWINE, JUTE ROPE, JUTE SOIL SAVER, RAW JUTE , JUTE TAPE & JUTE WEBBING etc with good reputations. We have our own spinning & composite factors named as Shamsher Jute Mills Ltd and Madina Jute Industries ltd, located at Norshingdi, about 50 km from our office / Dhaka.
